This is a private memorial to a school — Nanchang No. 15 Middle School, a key high school of Jiangxi Province founded in 1958. From 2022 to 2025, Xiong Xinchen recorded its playground, corridors, sunsets and snow with a phone camera.

I · ABOUT

The School

Nanchang No. 15 Middle School was founded in 1958. It is a public combined junior-and-senior high school directly under the Nanchang Education Bureau, a Key High School of Jiangxi Province and a Provincial Model Campus for Civility, sitting on the shore of Qingshan Lake. According to public records, the school now runs 51 classes (23 junior high, 28 senior high) with more than 2,600 students.

In 2012, approved by the Nanchang Municipal Government, the school merged with the former Nanchang No. 30 Middle School, whose campus became the junior-high site. The junior section was renamed “Qingshanhu Campus of Nanchang No. 2 Middle School Education Group” in 2016, and in 2017 the senior section opened “No. 2 Middle School classes” staffed and resourced jointly with Nanchang No. 2 Middle School. According to public information from 2024, the school has an authorized staff of 154; the principal is Xiong Yi.

On campus, red-brick teaching buildings mingle with camphor and cedar trees, and the old water tower rises above the treetops. The track field, basketball courts, tennis courts and library are all there — and the sports venues open to the neighbourhood.

“Dedication builds virtue; diligent study cultivates the self.” — School motto

II · HISTORY

A Brief Chronicle

Only the years on record are listed — historical restraint is itself a form of sincerity.

1958

Nanchang No. 15 Middle School is founded. For the next six decades and more, the campus never leaves the shore of Qingshan Lake.

2012

Merged with the former Nanchang No. 30 Middle School; the old No. 30 campus becomes the junior-high site, making the school a combined junior-and-senior high directly under the Municipal Education Bureau.

2016

The junior section is officially renamed “Qingshanhu Campus of Nanchang No. 2 Middle School Education Group”, joining the group-school system.

2017

The senior section opens “No. 2 Middle School classes”, with teachers dispatched and resources shared by Nanchang No. 2 Middle School.

III · GALLERY

Light & Shadows

I

The Gate & the Plaza

First impressions: the gate, the flagpole, the open plaza

The five silver characters spelling “Nanchang Shiwuzhong” (Nanchang No. 15) on the pink-brick gate posts were the coordinates of a thousand comings and goings; the plaza in summer break is empty, and the crescent sculpture in front of the office building still points at the sky.

II

Buildings & Classrooms

Where learning happened

Red-brick buildings with white window bands, classrooms hung with the school motto, corridors with a “Senior Grade 1” nameplate — and, in the last month before graduation, a sun-flooded open study space.

III

Seasons · Trees & Light

Spring camphor · summer glow · autumn dusk · winter snow

The camphor trees put out red-and-green leaves at the spring equinox; June sunsets dye the water tower into a silhouette; the street lamps come on one by one in late autumn; and the snowstorm of January 22, 2024 bent the cedars — and quieted the whole campus.

IV

The Playground

The terracotta of youth

At 7:18 on the morning of August 31, 2022, the red track welcomed a new senior-high student’s first photograph; over the following three years, the basketball courts, tennis courts and flagpole plaza collected every break and every dusk.

V

The Arts Festival

Young dreams · vivid lives

On May 8, 2023, the “Young Dreams, Vivid Lives” campus arts festival opened: a check-in desk stood on the plaza, and the school motto, teachers’ creed and philosophy were printed on the backdrop.

VI

The Signature Wall

The eve of Gaokao · a way to say goodbye

June 6, 2025, the day before Gaokao — a signature wall rose on the plaza. A wall full of names: the liveliest farewell of those three years.

IV · LANDMARK

A Water Tower’s Way of Remembering

Every old campus keeps one structure older than all its buildings. At No. 15, it is this old water tower standing above the treetops.

It stopped supplying water long ago, yet still catches every sunrise and sunset on time. For three years it appeared at the right of the classroom window, at the end of the track, in the upward glance on the walk home — a silent bell-keeper, remembering for us: classes beginning, classes ending, the seasons, graduation.

VI · EPILOGUE

Words

In three years we saw too many mornings and evenings here — dawn light under the flagpole, sunset at the end of the corridor, snow-laden cedar branches, the empty plaza of summer break. Only after graduating did we understand: your alma mater is the campus that you begin to miss without end only after you leave.
This page is dedicated to the red-brick buildings, the camphor trees and the old water tower by Qingshan Lake — and to everyone who spent their youth at No. 15.

— Class of 2025
